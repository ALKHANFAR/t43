Privacy Policy

Siyadah AI · Operated by شركة عشر أبعاد لخدمات الأعمال · Effective date to be set after approval

What this policy covers

This policy explains the public early-access form and Siyadah's private pilot, including the optional Gmail connection. Joining the waitlist does not connect your Google account.

Early-access requests

The public form asks for your name, email address, phone number and country code, role, and optionally your company. It also submits the page URL, referring page and time. We use this information to handle your request and contact you about early access. The form sends the submission to a cloud workflow service that routes the request for our team; if it fails, your browser may open an email draft addressed to our team with the details you entered.

Gmail connection and sending

Only when you choose to connect Gmail in the private pilot do we request the Google gmail.send permission. It permits Siyadah to send email from that Google account when you request a specific send. This feature does not request permission to read your inbox, contacts, or existing messages. We receive an authorization result and create a connection assigned to your company in our hosted workflow service. For a requested send, the recipient, subject and message content are processed by Siyadah and that service and sent to Google; we record the request and its result so you can see what happened. Google may retain sent messages under your own Google account settings.

We use data obtained through this Google connection only to provide the sending feature and related security and support. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. We do not disclose it to unrelated third parties. Infrastructure providers process only the data needed to host and operate this service under our direction.

Storage, security and retention

Connection credentials are kept in the workflow service while your Gmail connection is active. We use HTTPS for transmission, access controls that separate company workspaces, and a configured credential-encryption service. The service and its infrastructure may process or store data outside Saudi Arabia. Request and result records are kept as needed to provide your company history, troubleshoot runs and meet security or legal needs; they may also appear in operational logs and backups. We do not promise immediate removal of records in every backup. We review and delete data no longer needed when you close the service or request deletion, subject to legal or security retention requirements.

Disconnecting Gmail in Siyadah removes its active connection and stops future use through Siyadah. You may also revoke Siyadah's access in your Google account. Neither action recalls email already sent or automatically removes a copy kept in the recipient's mailbox. To ask us to review or delete remaining Siyadah records, contact us below.

Your choices and questions

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, or ask about a complaint, by writing to info@siyadah-ai.com. We will review the request and explain any data we must retain. We will update this policy before materially changing how we use Google data and request new permission where required.

ربط Gmail والإرسال

فقط عندما تختار ربط Gmail في التجربة الخاصة نطلب صلاحية Google gmail.send. تتيح لسيادة إرسال رسالة من حسابك عندما تطلب إرسالًا محددًا. لا تطلب هذه الميزة إذن قراءة صندوق الوارد أو جهات الاتصال أو الرسائل الموجودة. نتلقى نتيجة التفويض وننشئ اتصالًا خاصًا بشركتك في خدمة تشغيل التدفقات التي نستضيفها. وعند طلب الإرسال، تعالج سيادة وهذه الخدمة المستلم والموضوع ومحتوى الرسالة وترسلها إلى Google، ونسجل الطلب ونتيجته لتعرف ما حدث. قد تحتفظ Google بالرسائل المرسلة وفق إعدادات حسابك لديها.

نستخدم البيانات التي نحصل عليها عبر اتصال Google لتقديم ميزة الإرسال وما يرتبط بها من أمان ودعم فقط. لا نبيعها، ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات أو لتدريب نماذج ذكاء اصطناعي عامة، ولا نفصح عنها لأطراف غير مرتبطة بالخدمة. يعالج مزودو البنية التحتية البيانات اللازمة لاستضافة هذه الخدمة وتشغيلها تحت توجيهنا.
