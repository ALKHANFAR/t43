Early access

The public website lets you request a place on Siyadah AI's early access list. Submitting a request does not activate a product account or connect an external tool. The website describes a private beta; access and available features are communicated separately.

Optional tool connections

If Gmail connection becomes available in your Siyadah account, you choose whether to authorize it through Google. The current reviewed implementation is designed for sending messages you request. The permissions and available actions shown to you at connection time govern what the connected account can do. You may disconnect the tool in Siyadah or revoke access in Google.

Questions

Contact info@siyadah-ai.com about the website or early access. See the Privacy Policy for information about personal data.
