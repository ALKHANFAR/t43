Privacy Policy

Siyadah AI · Effective 4 September 2026 · العربية

What we collect

When you reserve a seat we collect your name, work email, phone number, company (optional) and role, plus the page you came from. Nothing else. No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics cookies.

Why

To contact you about early access — and only that. We send one email when your wave opens.

Where it lives

Submissions are stored in a private spreadsheet and notification inbox controlled by Siyadah AI, transmitted over HTTPS through our automation provider (Activepieces). We do not sell or share your data.

Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and the GDPR, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. Email privacy@siyadah-ai.com and we act within 30 days.

Your business data, later

When you use the platform, your customer lists, financials and conversations stay yours. They are never used to train any model — ours or anyone else's.
