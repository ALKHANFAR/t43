02 — How it works

Three minutes from overwhelmed to operational.

No integrations to configure. No workflows to map. You talk to Siyadah the way you'd talk to someone joining on their first day.

Step 01

Tell it what you need

In plain language. No technical vocabulary, no explaining how your systems are wired together.

One sentence is enough "Follow up with every new lead within five minutes, chase invoices more than seven days late, and answer the support questions we get over and over."
Step 02

Siyadah reads your company, builds the team, and shows you the plan

Give it your website or Instagram. It reads everything — services, prices, policies, the questions customers ask, even your tone — and turns it into what your employees know from day one. Then it creates a dedicated agent per department, connects to the tools you already use, and shows you exactly what it intends to do before it does any of it. You approve, or you adjust it with a sentence.

Step 03

Go live, then go home

The team starts immediately. Leads get followed up. Invoices get chased. Customers get answers. You go back to the work only you can do — and you get your evenings back.
